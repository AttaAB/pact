# Pact — Discovery Assumptions

**Stage:** Product Discovery  
**Status:** Initial hypotheses — not yet validated

## 1. Problem Hypothesis

I suspect that people regularly make commitments to themselves or others but sometimes struggle to follow through. Existing solutions, such as reminders, group chats, and habit trackers, might not provide the motivation or accountability they need. I hypothesize that adding a social/gamified element to promises will encourage ppl to follow through (due to visibility, evidence, and whimsy :)

## 2. Product Idea

**idea:** Pact is a playful social accountability product intended to help people follow through on commitments to themselves and other. The goal is to investigate whether making accountability more social, visible, and enjoyable could help people keep their commitments, because if we're being honest... ppl break promises/commitments to themselves and others far too often (myself included)

**Core question:** Is this a meaningful enough problem that people would actively seek a better solution?

## 3. High-Level User Story

> As someone who makes commitments to myself or my friends, I want a simple way to stay accountable so that I'm more likely to follow through on what I've agreed to do.

*This is my interpretation of a potential user need, not something validated by users.*

## 4. Initial Assumptions

### About Target Users (to be revised)

- **A1:** University students and young adults (roughly 17–25) are a relevant target audience.
- **A2:** These users make commitments frequently enough that managing them could be valuable.
- **A3:** Some users are comfortable involving friends in their accountability.

### About the Problem

- **A4:** Forgetting, lacking motivation, or lacking accountability contributes to missed commitments to one's self and others (big no no).
- **A5:** Existing solutions do not adequately address at least some of these problems.

### About Potential Value

- **A6:** Social accountability could improve motivation and follow-through.
- **A7:** Gamification could make following through more enjoyable.
- **A8:** Users may find enough additional value in Pact to adopt it over their existing methods.

## 5. Questions to Investigate

**Users & Problem**
- Who actually experiences this problem?
- What types of commitments do they struggle with?
- How often does this happen?
- Why do they fail to follow through?
- How do they currently manage commitments?

**Market & Alternatives**
- What tools or methods are people currently using?
- What frustrates them about these solutions?
- What, if anything, would motivate them to switch?

**Potential Solutions**
- Would involving friends make accountability more effective or uncomfortable?
- Would playful features create lasting value or just initial excitement?
- What would an ideal experience look like from the user's perspective?
