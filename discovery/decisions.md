# Pact — Product Decisions

This document tracks the key product decisions made throughout discovery, including the reasoning, supporting evidence, alternatives considered, and trade-offs.

## 1. Target User & Problem

- Which specific users are we prioritizing for our initial product?
- What specific problem are we solving for them?
- Should Pact initially focus on personal commitments, interpersonal commitments, or both?

## 2. Value Proposition & Differentiation

- What value will Pact provide that users aren't already getting from existing solutions?
- Why would someone choose Pact over their current methods (e.g., reminders, group chats, habit trackers)?

## 3. MVP Scope & Prioritization

- What is the smallest version of Pact that can deliver meaningful value and test our core hypothesis?
- Which features are essential to that experience, and which can we intentionally exclude?
- What trade-offs are we making by prioritizing certain features or use cases over others?

## 4. Core User Experience

- What should the fundamental user journey look like, from making a commitment to determining its outcome?
- How should social accountability work, including friend participation, consent, and confirmation of commitments?
- What privacy or trust considerations must the initial experience address?

## 5. Technical Feasibility

- Can we realistically build the proposed MVP with our available time, skills, and resources?
- What are the biggest technical uncertainties or constraints?
- What technical trade-offs might affect the product experience or MVP scope?

## 6. Validation & Success

- What evidence would give us enough confidence to move from discovery into development?
- How will we determine whether our MVP actually solves the problem?
- What measurable user behaviours or outcomes would indicate success?
